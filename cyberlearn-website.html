ℹ️ About CyberLearn›

CyberLearn — Field File System
CyberLearn takes you from your very first Linux command to job-ready cybersecurity fundamentals. Seven structured tracks — Linux & Termux, Networking, Reconnaissance & OSINT, Web App Security, System Security, Bug Bounty Methodology, and Reporting & Professional Practice — walk you through 39 hands-on lessons, each paired with a real supporting video.

Every lesson comes with a built-in sandboxed practice terminal so you can try real command syntax safely, a notes & to-do system to track what you're learning, and progress tracking that turns study into XP, streaks, and badges. Finish every track and claim a free Certificate of Completion.

CyberLearn is completely free to use, works offline once your progress is loaded, and is built for one goal: turning a total beginner into someone genuinely confident with cybersecurity fundamentals.
